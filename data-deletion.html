Data Deletion Instructions

Control your data

If you want to delete your stored learner data, please follow the steps below. In a production app, this process should be supported by a secure backend and authenticated account deletion flow.

  1. Open the browser settings for this application.
  2. Delete site data or local storage for Digital Life Academy.
  3. Clear saved browser history for this domain if you also want to remove cached activity.
  4. Sign out of any associated account and request account deletion through the official support channel.

For a full production implementation, the platform should offer a secure “Delete My Account” option in the account settings page and a backend process that removes profile data, lesson progress, and associated records.
