Educational Disclaimer

Learning support, not replacement care

This platform provides educational resources, reflective activities, and digital-life guidance. The content is meant to support learning and healthy habits, not to replace professional advice.

When to seek help

For mental health concerns, bullying, online harm, emotional distress, or serious safety issues, users should speak to a trusted adult, teacher, school counsellor, parent, or a relevant professional service.

Important reminder

Our content is general educational material and should be used with judgment. If a user is facing urgent or complex personal circumstances, they should seek qualified support from the appropriate professional or service provider.
